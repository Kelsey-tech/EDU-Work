The Process

Three steps to your next opportunity

Whether you're a student looking for work or a company looking for talent, EDU-WORK makes the process simple.

01

Create Your Profile

Sign up as a student or company. Students add their skills, education and experience. Companies set up their hiring profile and post jobs in minutes.

02

Browse & Match

Students browse open internships and entry-level roles filtered by location and job type. Our system surfaces the most relevant opportunities for your profile.

03

Apply & Get Hired

Submit your application with a cover letter directly on the platform. Companies review applicants, accept or reject, and the student is notified instantly.

FAQ

Common questions

Is EDU-WORK free to use?

Yes — EDU-WORK is completely free for students. Companies can sign up and post jobs at no cost during the current phase.

What kinds of jobs are on the platform?

EDU-WORK focuses on internships and entry-level positions designed for students and recent graduates. You can filter by job type and location.

How do I know if my application was seen?

Every application shows a status — Pending, Accepted, or Rejected — which updates in real time as the company reviews your application.

Can I apply for multiple jobs?

Yes. You can apply to as many jobs as you like. All your applications are tracked in the My Applications section of your dashboard.

How do companies post a job?

After registering as a company, go to your dashboard and click Post a Job. Fill in the title, location, description, job type and requirements — it takes under 2 minutes.
