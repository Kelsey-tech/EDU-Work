About EDU-WORK

We're on a mission to close the gap between education and employment — starting with South Africa's students.

Who We Are

Built to bridge the gap between students and opportunity

EDU-WORK was created because too many talented students struggle to find their first job — not because they lack skills, but because they lack access. At the same time, companies spend weeks trying to find the right entry-level talent.

We built a platform that solves both problems at once. Students get a clear, simple way to find internships and entry-level jobs. Companies get a direct line to motivated, qualified candidates — without the noise.

We started in Johannesburg and we're growing across South Africa.

Our Mission

To help every student in South Africa find meaningful work experience — and help companies find the talent that will shape their future.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted career platform for students and graduates across Africa.
